B. Course Categories 
IT & Computer Courses 
1. AI for Office Productivity 
Learn how to use Artificial Intelligence tools to improve everyday office tasks and productivity. This 
course covers AI-assisted document preparation, email writing, research, data organization, 
meeting support, workflow automation, and other practical workplace applications. 
2. AI for Data & Excel Mastery 
Develop practical skills in using AI with Microsoft Excel for data organization, analysis, formulas, 
reporting, and visualization. Learners will explore how AI can simplify complex Excel tasks and 
improve accuracy and productivity. 
3. AI for Presentation & Content Creation 
Learn how to use AI tools to create professional presentations and engaging digital content. The 
course covers AI-assisted content writing, presentation planning, visual ideas, slide creation, 
editing, and content improvement. 
4. Generative AI Basics & Prompt Engineering 
Get introduced to Generative AI and learn how to communicate effectively with AI tools. The course 
covers AI fundamentals, prompt writing techniques, ChatGPT and similar AI tools, content 
generation, problem-solving, and practical workplace applications. 
5. AI for Graphic Design 
Learn how Artificial Intelligence can support modern graphic design workflows. Learners will explore 
AI-powered design tools for generating ideas, images, layouts, visual concepts, editing, and creative 
content while developing practical design skills. 
6. Computer Operation – Basic to Advanced 
Build strong computer skills from basic to advanced levels. This course covers computer 
fundamentals, operating systems, file management, typing, Microsoft Office applications, internet 
and email usage, digital tools, and essential workplace computing skills. 
7. Computer Operation – Level 2 / Level 3 
Develop competency in computer operation and office applications through structured practical 
training. Learners will gain skills in document preparation, spreadsheets, presentations, internet 
applications, data handling, and workplace-oriented computer tasks. 
8. IT Support & Computer Servicing – Level 3 / Level 4 
Learn practical IT support and computer servicing skills, including hardware components, operating 
systems, installation, troubleshooting, maintenance, networking fundamentals, and technical 
support. The course prepares learners for entry-level IT support and servicing roles. 
9. Web Development – Level 3 
Learn the fundamentals of creating functional and responsive websites. The course introduces 
HTML, CSS, JavaScript fundamentals, web page structure, responsive design, basic development 
practices, and practical website projects. 
10. Web Design & Development for Freelancing – Level 2 
Develop practical web design and development skills with a focus on freelancing. Learners will work 
on website layouts, responsive pages, basic front-end development, client requirements, project 
practice, and essential freelancing concepts. 
11. Graphic Design & Freelancing 
Develop professional graphic design skills and learn how to apply them in the freelancing 
marketplace. The course focuses on design principles, typography, color, branding, social media 
graphics, promotional materials, portfolio development, and basic client communication. 
12. Graphic Design – Level 2 / Level 3 / Level 4 
Build graphic design competencies progressively through practical training. Depending on the level, 
learners develop skills in design fundamentals, image editing, vector graphics, layout design, 
branding, digital graphics, and professional design projects. 
13. Graphic Design for Freelancing – Level 3 / Level 4 
Learn advanced graphic design skills with a strong focus on freelancing and real-world projects. 
Learners practice creating professional design solutions, developing portfolios, understanding client 
requirements, completing projects, and preparing for freelance work. 
14. Digital Marketing – Level 3 / Level 4 / Level 5 
Develop practical digital marketing skills for modern businesses and online platforms. Training 
covers digital marketing fundamentals, social media marketing, content strategy, online promotion, 
audience engagement, campaign planning, analytics, and advanced digital marketing practices 
according to the level. 
15. 2D Animation & Motion Graphics 
Learn the fundamentals of 2D animation and motion-based visual communication. The course 
covers animation principles, character and object movement, storyboarding, visual storytelling, basic 
motion graphics, and practical animation projects. 
Professional & Trainer Development 
1. Competency-Based Assessment Methodology – Level 4 
This course is designed for professionals who want to develop the knowledge and practical skills required 
to conduct competency-based assessments. Learners will understand assessment planning, evidence 
collection, assessment tools, competency judgment, assessment documentation, feedback techniques, and 
quality assurance practices in competency-based assessment. 
2. Competency-Based Training & Assessment Methodology – Level 4 
This course prepares trainers and TVET professionals to design, deliver, and assess competency-based 
training programs. It covers CBT&A principles, session planning, learning facilitation, competency-based 
assessment, learner support, assessment documentation, moderation, validation, and continuous 
improvement of training and assessment practices. The course emphasizes practical application and 
professional competency in delivering quality training. 
Who Should Join? 
This course is suitable for trainers, instructors, assessors, teachers, TVET professionals, training 
coordinators, and individuals interested in developing competency-based training and assessment skills for 
professional growth. 
Healthcare & Caregiving 
SkillBridge IT Farm and Language Center offers practical, competency-based healthcare and caregiving 
courses designed to prepare learners for professional caregiving and primary healthcare support roles. 
These courses focus on essential caregiving knowledge, practical skills, safety, communication, and 
compassionate care. 
• Primary Health Care Services Level-2 
This course provides practical knowledge and skills in basic healthcare services and community 
health support. Learners will gain competency in personal hygiene, infection prevention and control, 
first aid, basic patient care, health awareness, nutrition, vital signs monitoring, and safe healthcare 
practices. 
• General Caregiving Level-2 
This course prepares learners to provide quality care and support for individuals in home and 
healthcare settings. It covers personal care, hygiene assistance, mobility support, communication, 
nutrition, patient safety, daily living assistance, and professional caregiving ethics. 
• Caregiving for Elderly Persons Level-3 
This course focuses on providing safe, respectful, and compassionate care for older adults. 
Learners will develop skills in elderly personal care, mobility assistance, medication support, 
nutrition, fall prevention, communication, emotional support, and promoting healthy ageing. 
• Caregiving for Infants, Toddlers & Children Level-3 
This course develops practical caregiving skills for infants and young children. Topics include child 
growth and development, feeding and nutrition, hygiene, safety, first aid, play-based learning 
support, emotional care, and creating a safe and healthy environment for children. 
• Caregiving for Persons with Special Needs Level-3 
This course prepares learners to support children and adults with physical, intellectual, 
developmental, or sensory disabilities. Learners will gain practical skills in personal care, 
communication, mobility assistance, daily living support, safety, inclusion, and providing 
compassionate, person-centered care. 
Who Should Join? 
These courses are suitable for individuals interested in healthcare and caregiving careers, home 
caregivers, support workers, community health workers, parents, and anyone who wants to develop 
professional caregiving skills through practical, competency-based training.

professional development, or overseas employment opportunities. The course covers Korean 
alphabet, grammar, vocabulary, conversation, listening, reading, writing, and workplace 
communication.
