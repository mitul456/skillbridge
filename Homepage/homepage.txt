Section 2: About SkillBridge (Short Introduction) 
• SkillBridge IT Farm and Language Center is a modern skill development and language training 
institute dedicated to preparing learners for successful careers through practical, competency
based education. 
We combine technology, language learning, and professional skills to help students become job
ready, freelancing-ready, and entrepreneurship-ready. Our training focuses on hands-on 
practice, real-world applications, and industry-relevant competencies, ensuring learners gain the 
confidence and skills needed to succeed in today’s competitive world. 
At SkillBridge, we believe that the right skills create the right opportunities. 
Build Skills. Grow Career. 
1. Institutional Training Approach 
At SkillBridge IT Farm and Language Center, we follow the CBT&A (Competency-Based Training & 
Assessment) approach, focusing not only on theoretical knowledge but also on developing the practical 
skills required to perform real-world tasks effectively. 
Our Training Approach Includes: 
• Competency-Based Learning focused on measurable skills and outcomes. 
• Training delivered by experienced and NSDA-associated trainers. 
• Small-batch learning with personalized guidance and mentoring. 
• Hands-on practice and project-based learning to build practical experience. 
• Regular assessment and performance evaluation to measure competency. 
• A learner-centered approach designed to build confidence, practical ability, and workplace 
readiness. 
2. Practical & Competency-Based Learning 
o At SkillBridge, we emphasize Learning by Doing. Learners gain practical experience by 
performing hands-on tasks, working on real-world projects, and applying their knowledge in 
practical situations. 
o What We Provide: 
▪ Practical, workshop-based learning with extensive hands-on activities. 
▪ Practice through real-world projects and case studies. 
▪ Training with industry-relevant software, tools, and technologies. 
▪ Regular feedback, practice, and competency assessment for continuous improvement. 
▪ Career-focused training designed to prepare learners for employment, freelancing, and 
entrepreneurship. 
o Suggested Website Highlight 
Learn by Doing. Build Real Skills. Grow Your Career.

Section 6: Language Courses 
SkillBridge IT Farm and Language Center offers practical English, Japanese, German, and Korean 
language courses for learners interested in international education, overseas employment, and professional 
opportunities. The courses focus on speaking, listening, reading, writing, grammar, vocabulary, 
pronunciation, and practical communication skills according to the learner’s level. 
Our language training is designed to help learners build the communication skills required for study, work, 
and everyday life abroad. We also provide general guidance on overseas career opportunities, language 
proficiency requirements, application procedures, and visa-related documentation where applicable. 
Learn a Language. Expand Your Opportunities.
